# LinkedIn Post Humanizer

Use this skill when the user wants a LinkedIn post written or rewritten. Produce drafts only; do not publish.

## Steps

1. Gather the point of the post, the audience, and any voice notes (casual, dry, technical). If they pasted a stiff draft, keep the facts and change the voice.
2. Start with a first line that a human would actually say — a concrete moment, a number they own, or a sharp claim — not `I'm thrilled to announce` or `In today's fast-paced world`.
3. Short paragraphs (1–3 lines). One idea per paragraph. A line break is not a substitute for a point.
4. Cut stock phrases: `excited to share`, `humbled`, `game-changer`, `leverage`, `it's not just X, it's Y`, `the future of work`, hashtag walls.
5. Prefer a specific story or lesson over a list of buzzwords. If there is no real story, write a clear opinion and say that is what it is.
6. Close with a question or a single next step, not a request to "like and repost" unless they asked for that.
7. Offer two drafts of different lengths (short ~800 characters, and a longer one). Optional: 3 hashtags, not 20.

## Rules

- Do not invent jobs, metrics, or quotes. Use `[insert]` for missing facts.
- Do not impersonate a real third party. Do not stuff keywords unnaturally.
- Keep claims defensible. If the source draft overclaims, tone it down and flag it.
